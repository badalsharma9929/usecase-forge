    How it works

    1. Researcher reads the site and builds a company profile.
    2. Strategist drafts use cases, each citing passages from the site.
    3. Critic scores the plan from 1 to 10 and sends weak plans back for revision.
    4. Dataset scout searches Hugging Face and Kaggle and keeps only relevant datasets.
    5. Reporter assembles a report you can download or share.

    A run usually takes 2 to 5 minutes. You can leave this page; the link in your address bar brings you back to the results.

    Built with LangGraph, Gemini and Vercel. Use cases are AI-generated starting points; validate them before you build.
